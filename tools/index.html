Quick checks

Small calculators for the back-of-envelope numbers the guides use. They need JavaScript; the formula under each one works on paper too.

Quick check: nameplate to watts

Without JavaScript: running watts ≈ volts × amps. A motor's starting watts are often 2–3× running (estimate).

Quick check: minimum drawdown and precharge

Without JavaScript: drawdown needed = GPM × minutes. Precharge = cut-in − 2 psi, set with the tank empty.

Quick check: power station runtime

Without JavaScript: hours ≈ Wh × 0.85 ÷ average watts. The 85% allows for inverter losses and is an estimate.

Quick check: will this circuit hold?

Without JavaScript: amps = watts ÷ volts. Keep continuous loads at or under 12 A (1,440 W) on 15 A, 16 A (1,920 W) on 20 A.

Quick check: snowblower passes

Without JavaScript: passes = driveway width ÷ (clearing width in ft × 0.85 for overlap), rounded up. Walking ≈ passes × length; at ~2 mph that's about 176 ft a minute (estimate).

Quick checks are estimates for planning. For full answers use the tools above, and confirm with nameplates and manufacturer charts.
