Open Safety

Five rules worth memorising

  1. Surge decides the generator. A well pump often starts at 2–3× its running watts estimate.
  2. Load to 80%. Generators, inverters and circuits all get the same margin: 12 A on a 15 A circuit, 16 A on 20 A.
  3. Start one motor at a time. Biggest surge first, about 10 seconds apart.
  4. A tank should give the pump about a minute. Drawdown ≥ GPM × 1 rule of thumb; precharge = cut-in − 2 psi.
  5. Never backfeed through a dryer outlet. Use a transfer switch or interlock installed by an electrician.
