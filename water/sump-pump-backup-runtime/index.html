Math: usable Wh = capacity × usable share (lithium station 85%, lead-acid 50% to protect the battery, then 85% inverter loss) ÷ average watts. Average = pump watts × share of time it runs. Estimates, not guarantees. Time a real cycle with a stopwatch; it beats our defaults.

Before the next storm

  • Test it now: unplug the main pump, pour a bucket in the pit, and watch the backup kick on.
  • Pump motors surge at startup. A station that can't cover the surge shuts off on the first cycle. Check the surge rating, not just Wh.
  • On city water with good pressure? A water-powered backup pump needs no battery. Not an option on a private well: no power, no pressure.
  • Generators go outside, 20 ft from doors and windows. Test your CO alarms: CO alarm planner.
