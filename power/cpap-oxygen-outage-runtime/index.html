Math: hours = battery Wh × efficiency ÷ average watts. DC cable 90%; AC outlet 85% plus ~10 W the station's inverter uses itself. Battery for your outage = watts × hours/day × days ÷ efficiency, plus a 20% reserve for battery age and cold rooms. Efficiencies, the 10 W and the 20% reserve are our planning assumptions, not measurements of any model.

Medical first. Do these before the next storm.

  • On oxygen? A battery is a bridge, not the plan. Keep backup cylinders from your oxygen supplier. Concentrator makers tell users to have an alternate oxygen source for outages.
  • Call your equipment supplier (DME) now. Ask which battery and DC cable they approve for your exact model.
  • Call your power company. Many keep a medical or life-support customer list for outage alerts. It does not promise faster repair, so you still need a backup.
  • CPAP: use the maker's DC cable or converter. ResMed recommends a DC-to-DC converter over an inverter.
  • The humidifier and heated tube use most of the power. Ask your doctor before changing therapy settings to stretch a battery.
  • Run one full night on the battery now. Real numbers beat ours.
  • Generators go outside, 20 ft from doors and windows. Check your CO alarms: CO alarm planner.
