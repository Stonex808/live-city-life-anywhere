Power stations and batteries

A portable power station can cover lights, Wi-Fi, phones and a fridge for a while, but most cannot start a 240 V well pump. Use the guide below for that decision, and this runtime check for a station you already have (or are shopping for).

Quick check: power station runtime

Without JavaScript: hours ≈ Wh × 0.85 ÷ average watts. The 85% allows for inverter losses and is an estimate.

Quick check: nameplate to watts

Without JavaScript: running watts ≈ volts × amps. A motor's starting watts are often 2–3× running (estimate).
