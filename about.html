About & disclosure

Who runs this hub, how numbers are chosen, and how the site earns money.

What this is

Live City Life, Anywhere is a free hub of guides and planning tools for people who want ordinary city comforts (reliable water, lights, heat, a working fridge) in places where the utility can’t be taken for granted: homes on private wells, off-grid cabins, rural properties at the end of a long power line, and anyone who loses power every storm.

The tools live on their own small sites so each one loads fast and does one job: the Well Pump Generator Planner, the Well Pressure Tank Sizer and the Space Heater Planner. The Snowblower Size Picker runs right here on the hub. This hub ties them together with guides.

How we handle numbers

  • Rules of thumb are labeled estimate or “rule of thumb”.
  • Specific figures name their source: the NEC 80% continuous-load guideline, EIA fuel heat contents and prices, CPSC safety guidance, Ready.gov, USDA, Amtrol’s published drawdown card (in the tank sizer).
  • We don’t print prices, product ratings or statistics we can’t source. Fuel prices live in the Space Heater Planner, which cites the EIA release it uses.
  • Your nameplate, your bill and your manufacturer’s chart always beat our defaults.

Not professional advice

Everything here is planning information. It is not professional electrical, plumbing or well-system advice. Have a licensed electrician connect any generator to your home wiring, and a licensed well contractor or plumber work on your well system.
