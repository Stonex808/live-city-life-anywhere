Rules: USDA/FoodSafety.gov say a closed fridge keeps food safe about 4 hours; a full closed freezer about 48 hours, half full about 24. Toss perishables held above 40°F for 2+ hours. Thawed freezer food with ice crystals or at 40°F or below can be refrozen. The 25%/50% cuts for opened doors are our cautious planning assumption, not a USDA number. A thermometer reading beats the clock. When in doubt, throw it out.

Keep or toss: fridge items

Summary of USDA's refrigerated-food chart. Never taste food to check it. Smell and looks don't tell you about bacteria.

Do this now

  • Keep the doors shut. Every opening dumps cold air.
  • Past 4 hours with no end in sight? Move milk, meat, leftovers and eggs to a cooler packed with ice. Keep it at 40°F or colder.
  • USDA: 50 lb of dry ice holds a full 18 cu ft freezer for about 2 days. Handle dry ice with gloves and keep the room ventilated.
  • Group frozen food together. It stays cold longer.
  • Running a generator? Outside only, 20 ft from doors and windows. Check your CO alarms: CO alarm planner.
