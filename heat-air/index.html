Quick check: will this circuit hold?

Without JavaScript: amps = watts ÷ volts. Keep continuous loads at or under 12 A (1,440 W) on 15 A, 16 A (1,920 W) on 20 A.

Quick check: snowblower passes

Without JavaScript: passes = driveway width ÷ (clearing width in ft × 0.85 for overlap), rounded up. Walking ≈ passes × length; at ~2 mph that's about 176 ft a minute (estimate).
