Math: usable Wh = capacity × 85% (inverter loss) ÷ average watts. Average = furnace watts × share of time it runs + extras. Estimates, not guarantees. Your nameplate or a plug-in meter beats our defaults.

Before you plug anything in

  • Most furnaces are hardwired. Have a licensed electrician add a furnace transfer switch or inlet. No back-feeding through an outlet.
  • Newer furnaces can need a neutral-bonding plug or a station with a bonded neutral to light. Check the manual.
  • Fuel generators go outside, 20 ft from doors and windows. Running gas heat? Test your CO alarms: CO alarm planner.
  • Turning the thermostat down a few degrees lowers how often it runs, so the battery lasts longer.
